Explain Pain

An advanced course with Incitus on chronic pain: pain classifications, neurophysiological explanatory models, and educational, behavioural interventions. This insight into how an overstimulated nervous system keeps pain going fits seamlessly with my approach within the Watson Headache Method for chronic headache and migraine, and strengthens how I explain things to patients.

Wonne Back · 14 Dec 2025 · 1 minute read

Basic Course Jaw and Headache

A course with NIVS on the connection between jaw, neck and headache complaints, providing a solid foundation in examining and treating temporomandibular disorders (TMD) and the headaches that come with them. Exactly the combination that benefits many of my headache patients, alongside the Watson Headache Method and Dry Needling.

Wonne Back · 9 Nov 2024 · 1 minute read

Breathing in Practice

A course with The Health Fundament on recognising dysfunctional breathing patterns and the right breathing interventions to match. A disrupted breathing pattern often overloads the same accessory breathing muscles in the neck and shoulders that also play a role in headache and neck complaints – knowledge I now apply directly in treatment.

Wonne Back · 25 Oct 2024 · 1 minute read

Sleep Problems

A course with Viaperspectief on recognising and treating sleep problems. Poor sleep and pain complaints influence each other both ways, and especially with headache and migraine, a disrupted sleep rhythm is often a direct trigger – something I can now guide patients on more specifically.
